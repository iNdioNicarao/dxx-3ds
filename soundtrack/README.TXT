Descent 1 - Roland SC-55 Soundtrack (MP3)
=========================================
Recordings by Brandon Blume (https://sc55.duke4.net/)
Source archive: https://sc55.duke4.net/mp3/descent1_mp3.zip

Special thanks to GBAtemp user bakuDD for recommending this soundtrack pack
and providing the direct resource link to enhance the 3DS port.

Installation on Nintendo 3DS:
- Extract all .mp3 files directly into:
  sdmc:/3ds/d1/mp3/
- The game will automatically play matching tracks (e.g. game01.mp3 for level 1).
